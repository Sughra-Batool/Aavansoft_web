Support delivery standards

Service descriptions alone do not establish operational capability. Define the delivery model and how performance will be measured.

StandardWhat to define
Service scopeSupported systems, users, devices, applications and exclusions.
AvailabilitySupport hours, holidays, on-call coverage and service windows.
Response timeTarget time to acknowledge incidents by severity.
Resolution targetsTarget restoration or resolution times, dependencies and exceptions.
EscalationTechnical ownership, escalation paths and management notification.
SecurityAccess restrictions, identity verification, logging and incident handling.
ReportingTicket volumes, recurring incidents, resolution performance and availability.
ImprovementRoot-cause elimination, preventive maintenance and performance reviews.
Important distinction: Response time is not resolution time. Define and report them separately.

04 / Delivery principles

Engineering decisions should be evidence-led.

Aavansoft's delivery principles connect technology choices to validation, governance and measurable outcomes. Project-specific evidence and acceptance criteria are documented for each engagement.

✓
Evidence before claims

Establish baselines, benchmarks and acceptance criteria before claiming improvements.

✓
Architecture before tools

Select technologies according to workload requirements, not technology trends.

✓
Validation before deployment

Evaluate accuracy, robustness, uncertainty and limitations before operational use.

✓
Security and governance by design

Apply appropriate access controls, privacy safeguards, auditability and oversight.

✓
Measurable business value

Connect technical deliverables to operational, financial, scientific or strategic outcomes.

06 / Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about Aavansoft.

These answers summarize the service catalogue. Company-specific details are confirmed per engagement.

What services does Aavansoft provide?

The catalogue covers data engineering, governance, analytics, AI and machine learning, graph and causal analytics, scientific computing, optimization, MLOps, UI/UX design and technical support.

What does the data stack include?

It spans data infrastructure, governance, analytics, AI and machine learning, graph and knowledge systems, scientific computing, decision intelligence, optimization, data products and managed AI operations.

What technical support areas are listed?

Service desk assistance, infrastructure and cloud support, applications and enterprise systems, data and AI operations, cybersecurity, monitoring, backup and recovery, and managed IT services.

How does Aavansoft approach delivery quality?

The principles are evidence before claims, architecture before tools, validation before deployment, security and governance by design, and measurable business value.

What is the difference between response time and resolution time?

Response time is the target time to acknowledge an incident. Resolution time is the target time to restore service or resolve the issue. A support agreement defines both separately, including dependencies and exceptions.

Which technologies are included in the technology stack?

The list includes programming languages, web and mobile technologies, scientific computing libraries, machine learning frameworks, scientific AI, graph technologies, data storage, distributed systems, AI runtimes and autonomous AI components.

Does the service list guarantee a specific result or service level?

No. A service list alone does not establish operational capability. Scope, availability, response targets, resolution targets, security controls and reporting are agreed for each engagement.
