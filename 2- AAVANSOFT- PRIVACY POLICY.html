04

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

AAVANSOFT uses cookies and similar technologies to collect, store, and process information concerning website visits, browsing activity, user preferences, device characteristics, and website interactions.

Cookies are small data files stored on a visitor's browser or device when accessing a website. They enable websites to recognize devices, maintain functionality, remember preferences, and evaluate website usage.

Depending on implementation, AAVANSOFT may use the following categories:

4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support essential website operations, including security, navigation, session management, accessibility, and core functionality.

They cannot generally be disabled through website preference controls where they are strictly necessary for a service explicitly requested by the visitor.

4.2 Functional and Preference Cookies

These cookies retain selected preferences, settings, language choices, and other configuration information to provide a more consistent browsing experience.

4.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies help evaluate website traffic, visitor behaviour, page interactions, navigation patterns, performance, and technical issues.

The resulting information may be used to understand website usage, identify operational improvements, and enhance digital performance.

4.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

Where implemented, these technologies may be used to measure promotional effectiveness, understand visitor interests, and support relevant marketing activities.

Such technologies shall be activated only in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

4.5 Third-Party Cookies

Certain website functionalities, analytics services, embedded content, or external integrations may involve cookies placed by third-party providers.

These providers may process technical or usage information under their respective privacy policies and contractual arrangements.

AAVANSOFT does not control the independent data processing practices of third-party providers.

4.6 Cookie Consent and Management

Visitors may manage their cookie preferences through available website consent controls or their browser settings.

Where required by applicable law, non-essential cookies shall not be activated before the visitor provides valid consent.

Visitors may withdraw or modify their consent at any time through the available cookie preference mechanism.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality, personalization, or access to specific features.

4.7 Cookie Retention

Cookies may be classified as session cookies, which expire when the browsing session ends, or persistent cookies, which remain stored for a defined period or until deleted.

Retention periods shall depend on the cookie's purpose, configuration, and applicable legal requirements.

Visitors are encouraged to review their browser settings and available cookie preferences periodically.
